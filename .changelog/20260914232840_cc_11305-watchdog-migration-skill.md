The `ckeditor-v49-migration` skill: moving a project onto CKEditor 5 v49, from any earlier version.

It covers the removal of the Watchdog — in plain JavaScript and in the React, Vue and Angular integrations — and routes to the migration guides for what to change in each one. It reports the decisions that have no mechanical answer, such as whether to recreate a crashed editor, rather than making them.
